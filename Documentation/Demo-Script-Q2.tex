\section{Calibration}
\noindent\textbf{Presenter:} Pieter\\
\noindent\textbf{Estimated Time:} 2 minutes

\bigskip
\noindent\textit{Script:}

\begin{itemize}[itemsep=-3pt]
\item open group
\item explain why calibrate
\item do empty and full
\end{itemize}

In order for the product to be useful to every possible customer, a calibration sequence is required when setting it up. This calibration feature is also available at any time if the user purchases a new filter. Setting the calibration requires an empty and a full value. 

We'll start with full. First, take the pitcher at a full level and place it on the base. Then navigate through the app to find the calibration buttons. Once the full pitcher is on the scale, press "Calibrate Full".  Next, pour out the water from the filter until it is all empty. Place the pitcher back on the scale and press "Calibrate Empty". Now the levels will be accurate to your specific water filter.
