``Has this ever happened to you? You come home after a long day, you're thirsty, and you go to grab the water filter pitcher---only to find it completely empty. Nobody refilled it.
This is a common problem in shared living spaces---dorms, family homes, offices. Everyone uses the filtered water, but nobody knows when it's running low until it's too late. We want a way to know if the container is out of water when we need water. The goal is if the container is empty, we inform users.
Our project hopes to solve this. We built a smart water level monitoring system that sits underneath your water filter pitcher, continuously tracking how much water is left, and sends a notification to everyone in the household when it's time to refill.''
\end{quote}
